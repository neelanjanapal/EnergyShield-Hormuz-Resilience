01 — The problem

Hormuz is an energy dependency problem, not only a shipping problem.

A sustained disruption can create fuel shortages, price volatility, operational downtime and pressure to fall back on carbon-intensive emergency supply. Businesses need a way to quantify exposure and build an alternative energy system before the crisis peaks.

⚠️

Supply exposure

Fuel availability and delivery uncertainty can threaten critical operations.

📈

Cost volatility

Energy price stress can quickly change the economics of existing operations.

🏭

Continuity risk

Rail, ports, industry and critical facilities cannot simply stop when supply is disrupted.

02 — The product

EnergyShield = energy risk intelligence + physical resilience.

The platform turns disruption signals and site data into an actionable resilience plan, then helps operate the assets during the disruption.

🛡️

Risk Dashboard

Risk score, days of fuel remaining, cost-at-risk, emissions baseline and critical-site exposure.

⚡

Resilience Designer

Compare BESS, hydrogen, renewable supply, grid diversification and hybrid configurations.

🤖

AI Operations

Forecast disruption, stress-test scenarios and recommend charge, discharge and hydrogen production decisions.

🧪

Hydrogen Layer

Use green hydrogen for long-duration storage and hard-to-electrify transport and industrial applications.

🔋

BESS Layer

Use batteries for fast response, peak shaving, grid support and short-duration continuity.

♻️

Oxygen Marketplace

Identify nearby offtake opportunities for oxygen produced during electrolysis.

04 — Workflow

Assess → size → approve → dispatch.

EnergyShield is designed to move from strategic planning to crisis operations without changing the underlying platform.

STEP 01Assess

Upload site, fuel, load and criticality data. AI creates a risk profile.

STEP 02Design

Set a resilience target such as 8h, 24h or 7d and compare solutions.

STEP 03Approve

Review CAPEX, OPEX, payback, emissions and continuity benefits.

STEP 04Dispatch

During disruption, AI recommends BESS, renewable and hydrogen actions.

06 — Environmental value

Resilience can also reduce fossil dependency.

EnergyShield prioritizes renewable-powered BESS and green hydrogen where technically and economically appropriate, while recognizing lifecycle, safety and electricity-source constraints.

Lower operational carbon

Replacing diesel generation with renewable electricity and BESS can reduce operational emissions.

Hard-to-electrify sectors

Hydrogen can support heavy transport and industrial applications where direct electrification is difficult.

Resource utilization

Electrolysis oxygen can potentially become a useful industrial co-product when local offtake exists.
